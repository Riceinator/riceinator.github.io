Currently, I am a quantitative rates trader at Deutsche Bank, where I am focusing on systematic trading for USD Linear Rates (UST Cash/Futures).

Previously, I was a machine learning engineer with over 3 years of experience at Deutsche Bank. I am experienced with statistics, natural language processing, sentiment analysis, deep learning, and more. I primarily worked with Python and its associated machine learning libraries. I worked in the trade surveillance group at Deutsche Bank, where I was building machine learning models to detect fraudulent financial activities. I was also involved in creating Generative AI-based software in order to increase operational efficiency. 

I am currently at the University of Pennsylvania studying for a Master of Applied Science in Computer Science, with a concentration in Artificial Intelligence. Previously, I received a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering with a concentration in Energy and a B.A. in Philosophy from Rutgers University – New Brunswick.
